合作伙伴基本标准Partner standards

公开标准，而非私下认证Open standards, not a seal of approval

  1. 提供真实名称、地址、网站和负责人联系方式。Provide your real name, address, website and a responsible contact person.
  2. 不宣传未经证实的自闭症治疗、治愈或健康功效。Make no unproven claims about treating or curing autism, or other health benefits.
  3. 清楚披露价格、退款、赞助、佣金和公益金额。Clearly disclose prices, refunds, sponsorships, commissions and any amount for a good cause.
  4. 取得作品、姓名、照片和个人故事的使用授权。Get permission before using anyone's work, name, photo or personal story.
  5. 尊重自闭症人士的沟通方式、隐私和选择。Respect autistic people's ways of communicating, their privacy and their choices.
  6. 愿意配合项目定期复核和纠正错误信息。Be willing to take part in regular reviews and to correct wrong information.

这不是认证。列入本栏目不代表医疗资质认证，也不代表本站为其全部产品和服务担保。购买或接受服务前，请自行核验价格、资质、合同和退费规则。This is not a certification. A listing is not a medical credential, and we don't vouch for all of a partner's products and services. Before buying or using a service, check prices, qualifications, contracts and refund policies yourself.
