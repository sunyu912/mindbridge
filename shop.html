商品详情页必备字段Anatomy of a product page

每个商品页面必须公开五组信息Five things every product page must show

  1. 1

    商品主体Who's behind it

    商品名称、制作者或品牌、实际销售方。Product name, maker or brand, and the actual seller.

  2. 2

    商品信息Product details

    材料、尺寸、适用年龄、安全提示及实物与图片差异。Materials, size, ages, safety notes and how it may differ from photos.

  3. 3

    交易信息Buying terms

    价格、税费、运费、配送时间和退换货规则。Price, tax, shipping, delivery time and returns.

  4. 4

    收入分配Revenue split

    创作者收入、项目运营费用、公益金额及受益方。Creator income, running costs, and the amount for a cause plus recipient.

  5. 5

    授权与隐私Consent & privacy

    作品授权、姓名和照片展示同意，未成年人需监护人同意。Usage rights, name and photo consent, and guardian consent for under-18s.

商品健康声明Health claims

好用的东西，不等于有疗效Helpful isn't the same as therapeutic

本商店商品不用于诊断、治疗、治愈或预防自闭症及其他健康状况。普通商品、艺术作品和感官友好用品可能改善个人使用体验，但不应被描述为对所有自闭症人士具有医疗效果。Products in this shop are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent autism or any other health condition. Everyday goods, artwork and sensory-friendly items may make things more comfortable for some people, but they should never be described as having a medical effect for all autistic people.
