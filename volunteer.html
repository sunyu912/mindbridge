志愿服务边界Boundaries

清楚的边界，让每个人都更安全Clear boundaries keep everyone safer

志愿者不提供诊断、心理咨询、治疗建议或危机干预。未经项目负责人和成人指导人批准，志愿者不得与未成年人进行私人一对一联系，不得索取诊断记录、学校文件、住址或其他不必要的敏感信息。Volunteers do not provide diagnosis, counselling, treatment advice or crisis support. Without approval from the project lead and the adult advisor, volunteers may not contact minors privately one-to-one, and may not ask for diagnostic records, school documents, home addresses or any other unnecessary sensitive information.

涉及线下活动、儿童、资金、商品或个人故事的任务，由成人指导人审核，并遵守当地法律、学校规则和合作机构要求。Tasks involving in-person events, children, money, products or personal stories are advisor-reviewed and follow local law, school rules and partner requirements.

志愿者不会做的事What volunteers don't do

  • 不提供诊断No diagnosis
  • 不提供心理咨询或治疗建议No counselling or treatment advice
  • 不提供危机干预No crisis intervention
  • 未经批准，不与未成年人私人一对一联系No private one-to-one contact with minors without approval
  • 不索取诊断记录、学校文件、住址或其他不必要的敏感信息No requests for diagnostic records, school documents, addresses or other unnecessary sensitive information
  • 不以本站名义提供私人咨询No private consulting in MindBridge's name

本站不提供危机干预。如果你或他人面临立即危险，请拨打 911；在美国可拨打或短信联系 988。This site does not provide crisis support. If anyone is in immediate danger, call 911; in the U.S. you can call or text 988.

志愿者行为准则Code of conduct

尊重，是一切合作的起点Respect comes first

我们尊重自闭症人士的自主权、隐私、沟通方式和个人边界。We respect autistic people's autonomy, privacy, ways of communicating and personal boundaries.

用对方喜欢的称呼Use their words

使用本人偏好的称呼和表达。Use each person's preferred name and terms.

保护他人隐私Protect privacy

不公开他人的诊断、照片或故事。Never share anyone else's diagnosis, photos or story.

不作医疗承诺No medical promises

不承诺医疗或治疗效果。Never promise medical or treatment outcomes.

不借社群推销No self-promotion

不利用社群关系推销个人服务。Never use community relationships to sell personal services.

发现不安全行为、隐私泄露、虚假宣传或利益冲突，请立即联系项目负责人或成人指导人。Report unsafe behaviour, privacy leaks, false claims or conflicts of interest to the project lead or adult advisor right away.
